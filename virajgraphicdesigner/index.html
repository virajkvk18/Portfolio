METHODOLOGY

How I Build Visual Worlds

A rigorous 4-step creative pipeline that bridges raw research with production-grade execution across print and digital media.

PHASE 01

Visual Archaeology

Uncovering the brand's cultural DNA, auditing competitors, curating tone-setting moodboards, and mapping the emotional archetype.

PHASE 02

Grid & Typography

Establishing mathematical layout grids, intentional typographic hierarchies, and optical scale contrast that commands instant attention.

PHASE 03

Cohesive Systems

Formulating disciplined color tokens, vector logomarks, iconography, and versatile templates engineered for speed and brand coherence.

PHASE 04

Production Rollout

Flawless asset exports: print-ready CMYK with bleed margins, vector SVGs, and pixel-crisp 1080x1350 social carousels and mockups.
